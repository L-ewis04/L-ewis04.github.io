04 / INDIVIDUAL RESEARCHTHIRD YEAR · SOUTHAMPTON

Reconstructing 3D microscopy images

For my third-year research, I compared four ways to reconstruct 3D microscopy images from 2D measurements. I built a Python testing pipeline and a small visualisation app to compare reconstruction quality and runtime.

The Richardson–Lucy method with total-variation regularisation gave the best overall balance in my tests.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • NumPy
  • SciPy
  • Image processing
